Sunday, 1 November 2026 · Day 1 of 19

London Heathrow → Tokyo Haneda

Travel day
Sun1 Nov

London Heathrow → Tokyo Haneda

Flight BA5 · overnight flight

Depart London Heathrow at 11:45 on British Airways BA5. Scheduled arrival at Tokyo Haneda is 10:25 on Monday 2 November.

International flight: BA5, economy, 2 adults. Overnight onboard the aircraft. Flight details as provided by Inside Japan.

Mon2 Nov

Arrive in Tokyo

Arrival · shared shuttle

Arrive at Haneda Airport at 10:25. Take the pre-arranged shared shuttle from the airport to the Tokyo hotel; allow around one hour, with up to three other hotel stops.

Stay: Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier · 4 nights, 2–6 November. Double room, breakfast included. Mobile WiFi device rental (Softbank unlimited data, 18 days) is included in your itinerary; delivery to the first hotel.

Tue3 Nov

Tokyo · East Central Tokyo

Explore at your own pace

A free day to explore east-central Tokyo. Suggested areas in your itinerary include Asakusa and Sensō-ji, the Thunder Gate (Kaminarimon) and Nakamise shopping street.

Stay: Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier.

Wed4 Nov

Tokyo · West Central Tokyo

Explore at your own pace

A free day to explore west-central Tokyo. Choose neighbourhoods and sights to suit your energy and interests.

Stay: Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier.

Thu5 Nov

Mount Takao

Hike · around 3 hours

Hike Mount Takao. The proposal lists a three-hour hike; Follow the hiking arrangements provided by Inside Japan.

Stay: Mitsui Garden Hotel Jingugaien Tokyo Premier.

Fri6 Nov

Tokyo → Matsumoto

Limited Express train

Take the Limited Express train from Tokyo Shinjuku Station to Matsumoto. Journey time is approximately 2 hours 30 minutes. Tickets are listed as Limited Express, ordinary class. Visit Matsumoto Castle and the castle district.

Stay: Matsumoto Hotel Kagetsu · 1 night. Standard twin room in the main building (24 sqm), breakfast included.

Sat7 Nov

Matsumoto → Yabuhara → Narai

Train + Nakasendo walk

Train from Matsumoto to Yabuhara (ordinary fare tickets listed). Walk the Nakasendo route from Yabuhara to Narai: about 6.5 km / 3 hours. Luggage forwarding is noted as not included.

Stay: Echigoya, Narai · 1 night. Japanese-style twin-use room without private bathroom; breakfast and dinner included. Traditional inn with shared bathroom.

Sun8 Nov

Narai → Nagiso → Tsumago

Train + Nakasendo walk

Train from Narai to Nagiso (ordinary fare tickets listed). Walk from Nagiso to Tsumago, approximately one hour.

Stay: Daikichi, Tsumago · 1 night. Traditional Japanese-style twin-share room without private bathroom; breakfast and dinner included.

Mon9 Nov

Tsumago → Magome → Kyoto

Nakasendo walk + bus + trains

Walk from Tsumago to Magome: about 8 km / 3 hours. Take the bus from Magome to Nakatsugawa, then trains to Kyoto via Nagoya. The listed rail legs are Nakatsugawa–Nagoya (Limited Express, ordinary) and Nagoya–Kyoto (Nozomi Super Express, ordinary); fare tickets are also listed for Nakatsugawa–Kyoto.

Stay: Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Kyoto Premier · 5 nights, 9–14 November. Double room (25.5 sqm), breakfast included.

Tue10 Nov

Kyoto · guided day

Private guide service

Full-day private guide service in Kyoto. Your private guide will accompany you for the day; follow the arrangements provided by Inside Japan.

Stay: Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Kyoto Premier.

Wed11 Nov

Kyoto · arts and crafts

Culture and making

Arts and crafts experience in Kyoto, as planned. Enjoy the planned arts and crafts experience in Kyoto.

Stay: Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Kyoto Premier.

Thu12 Nov

Kyoto · Arashiyama option

Suggested sightseeing

Suggested alternative sightseeing day in Arashiyama. A flexible day to explore Arashiyama at your own pace.

Stay: Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Kyoto Premier.

Fri13 Nov

Kyoto · optional Nara day trip

Optional excursion

Optional day trip to Nara. Enjoy a day trip to Nara, travelling by train from Kyoto.

Stay: Solaria Nishitetsu Hotel Kyoto Premier.

Sat14 Nov

Kyoto → Takamatsu

Trains via Okayama

Train from Kyoto to Takamatsu via Okayama. Listed rail legs: Kyoto–Okayama on the Nozomi Super Express (ordinary) and Okayama–Takamatsu on the Limited Express (ordinary); fare tickets are also listed for Kyoto–Takamatsu. Visit Ritsurin Garden in Takamatsu.

Stay: Royal Park Hotel Takamatsu · 3 nights, 14–17 November. Double room, breakfast included.

Sun15 Nov

Naoshima art island

Ferries + bike rental

Ferry from Takamatsu to Naoshima (Miyanoura Port). Bike rental is listed as reservation-only. Visit the island and Chichu Art Museum, then take the ferry back to Takamatsu. Ferry tickets are not described as included; check the info-pack and pay arrangements.

Stay: Royal Park Hotel Takamatsu.

Mon16 Nov

Teshima art island

Ferries + island sightseeing

Ferry from Takamatsu to Teshima (Ieura Port). Visit Teshima Art Museum and explore the island, then return by ferry to Takamatsu. The return ferry journey takes around 35 minutes.

Stay: Royal Park Hotel Takamatsu.

Tue17 Nov

Takamatsu → Tokyo

Trains via Okayama

Train journey from Takamatsu to Tokyo via Okayama, approximately 4 hours 30 minutes. Listed legs: Takamatsu–Okayama Limited Express (ordinary), then Okayama–Tokyo Shinkansen Super Express (ordinary). Take a local train across Tokyo to Asakusa.

Stay: Asakusa View Hotel Annex Rokku · 2 nights, 17–19 November. Standard Queen room (29 sqm), breakfast included.

Wed18 Nov

Tokyo · final full day

Free time in the city

A final free day for anything missed at the start of the trip, last-minute shopping and a final evening in Tokyo.

Stay: Asakusa View Hotel Annex Rokku.

Thu19 Nov

Tokyo Haneda → London Heathrow

Shared shuttle + flight BA6

Shared shuttle from the hotel to Haneda Airport. The driver meets you in the hotel lobby; allow around one hour, with up to three other pick-ups. British Airways BA6 departs Haneda at 13:15 and is scheduled to arrive at London Heathrow at 19:10.

End of trip. Your shared shuttle takes you to Haneda for British Airways BA6.
